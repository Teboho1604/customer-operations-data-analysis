Findings

The analysis identified several patterns in SUV sales and customer ordering behaviour:

- Order frequency varies significantly across models, with some SUVs being ordered much more frequently than others.
- Higher order frequency does not automatically mean higher sales value. A model can be popular while generating less revenue per order than a lower-volume model.
- Customer purchasing behaviour differs across SUV models, showing that demand is not evenly distributed throughout the inventory.
- Ranking the models by order frequency makes the strongest-performing products easier to identify and can help management understand which SUVs require closer inventory monitoring.
- The analysis demonstrates how SQL can be used to transform raw sales data into useful business information rather than simply reporting individual transactions.

Business Insight

The results can support inventory and sales decisions by helping the business identify frequently ordered SUV models, monitor demand patterns, and prioritise stock according to observed customer demand.

Key Takeaway

The main takeaway is that sales data becomes more useful when it is analysed for patterns rather than viewed as individual records. Using SQL to rank and compare SUV order activity provides a simple but practical way to turn operational data into actionable business insights.
